Typography and Button Styling Guidelines

1. Typography Overview
   The game uses two main fonts to create a unified identity. Electronic Highway Sign SDF is used for titles and subpage headers. Bangers SDF is used for large button labels. Electronic Highway provides a technical spacecraft inspired look, while Bangers gives clear readable text for interactive buttons.

2. Title and Header Typography

2.1 Main Titles
Font: Electronic Highway Sign SDF
Size: 192.5 pt
Weight: Bold
Style: Italic
Softness: 0.3
Letter spacing: Slightly expanded if needed
Alignment: Centered horizontally
Use cases: main menu title, scene entry titles, page section headers

Notes: Main titles should feel dominant and sit near the top of the screen with plenty of space around them.

2.2 Subpage Titles
Font: Electronic Highway Sign SDF
Size: 192.5 pt
Weight: Bold
Style: Italic
Softness: 0.3
Alignment: Centered horizontally
Use cases: room page titles, minigame titles, section headings

Notes: Subpage titles match the style of main titles but appear lower in the hierarchy through layout and spacing.

3. Button Typography and Design

3.1 Button Title Text
Font: Bangers SDF
Size: 80 pt
Weight: Bold
Color: Light text such as white or light silver
Alignment: Centered within the button
Use cases: main menu buttons, large navigation buttons, key action buttons

Notes: Bangers SDF should only be used for button labeling and not for paragraphs or large titles.

3.2 Button Positioning
General rule: Most buttons should be centered horizontally on the screen.
Exceptions: Back buttons appear in the top left or bottom left corners. These should be smaller and unobtrusive.

3.3 Button Shape and Spacing
Buttons should use a rounded rectangle or similar shape.
Padding around text should be at least 24 to 32 pixels.
Maintain consistent button height across screens.
Leave at least 40 pixels of vertical spacing between large buttons.

4. Additional Recommendations

4.1 Readability
Keep title phrases short. Electronic Highway is bold and works best with limited text.

4.2 Consistency
Titles always use Electronic Highway Sign SDF. Button labels always use Bangers SDF. Do not mix these roles.

4.3 Softness
Keep Electronic Highway softness at 0.3 across all titles for consistent appearance.

4.4 Accessibility
Ensure strong color contrast, clear readability at different resolutions, and appropriate scaling of text on smaller screens.
