The Psyche mission is to study an asteroid, also called Psyche, which contains metal in its core.
Most asteroids are made of rock and ice, but Psyche is unique due to its metal-rich core. This is NASA’s first mission to study an asteroid like this.
The Psyche spacecraft was launched on October 13, 2023 from the Kennedy Space Center and is expected to reach the Psyche asteroid in July 2029.
The asteroid Psyche is located between Mars and Jupiter, about three times farther away from the Sun than Earth.
The asteroid Psyche was named after the Greek goddess of the soul, Psyche, who was born mortal and married to Cupid.
When the spacecraft arrives, it will orbit the asteroid for about two years, collecting data such as images and surface maps and sending them back to NASA.
Psyche is sometimes referred to as “16 Psyche” because it was the 16th asteroid to be discovered.
There is much less surface gravity on Psyche than on Earth, even less than on the Moon, so lifting a car would feel more like lifting a large dog.
