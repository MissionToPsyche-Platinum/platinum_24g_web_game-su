Muhammad:
Minigame Concept; Power Cell Calibration
Room Type: Engine Room
When the player’s character enters the ship’s engine room and
approaches the glowing control panel, a mini-game is triggered
to “calibrate the power cells.” The player sees a series of
energy bars fluctuating at different levels. Their task is
to click (or tap) each bar when it aligns with a highlighted 
target zone in the center of each bar. Each successful calibration
lights up part of the ship’s power system, 
while mistimed clicks reset that specific bar.
Once all bars are aligned, the ship’s lights flicker 
on and the mini-game ends, rewarding the player with
 a “Psyche Fact Card” and also restoring the ships power.

Remy:
Minigame Concept: Trajectory Correction Burn
Room Type: Control Room
This minigame takes place in the ship’s Control Room, where players handle 
critical mid-course adjustments as the spacecraft travels toward Psyche. 
When the player interacts with the task console, the screen displays a 
simplified navigation interface featuring a target trajectory line and a 
set of hands-on controls such as sliders, rotating knobs, or draggable 
directional arrows. Using mouse input, the player adjusts these 
controls to match the ship’s heading and thrust level with the highlighted 
target path. As they interact, the display updates in real time—trajectory 
arcs shift, angle indicators change, and feedback meters show how close the 
player is to achieving alignment. Once all values fall within the correct 
range, the game automatically initiates the correction burn, showing a brief 
engine-boost animation and awarding mission progress along with a Psyche-related 
fact card.

Shamitha:
Minigame Concept: Sample Clean-up Sweep
Room Type: Research/Data Lab
When the player enters the Research Lab, they notice a warning on the screen saying
that the sample area has small bits of debris floating around. Interacting with the
warning sign starts the mini-game. Inside the mini-game, different objects begin gently
falling or drifting downward. The safe sample pieces are marked clearly, while the
contaminants have small hazard icons. The player moves a small collection pad left
and right to catch only the safe pieces and avoid the contaminants. Each correct catch
fills up the “clean-up meter,” while catching a contaminant lowers it and triggers a 
warning sound. When the clean-up meter reaches 100%, the lab systems return to
normal, the warning clears, and the samples are ready to study. The player receives a 
Psyche Fact Card as a reward.
