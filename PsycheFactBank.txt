Psyche Fact Bank:

1. The Psyche mission is to study an asteroid, also called Psyche, which 
contains metal in the center.
2. Most asteroids are made of rock and ice, but Psyche is unique due to its
metal-rich core. This is NASA’s first mission to study an asteroid like this. 
3. The Psyche spacecraft was launched October 13, 2023 from the Kennedy Space
Center and is expected to reach the Psyche asteroid in July 2029.
4. The asteroid Psyche is located somewhere between Mars and Jupiter, about three
times farther away from the Sun than is Earth.
5. The asteroid Psyche was named after the Greek goddess of the soul, Psyche, who
was born mortal and married to Cupid. 
6. When the spacecraft arrives, it will be orbiting around the asteroid for about
 2 years collecting data - like taking pictures and mapping out the surface -  and
sending it back to NASA.
7. Psyche is sometimes referred to as “16 Psyche” because it is the 16th asteroid
to be discovered.
8. There is much less surface gravity on Psyche than there is on Earth. This is
even less than the moon, so lifting cup a car would feeling like lifting a large dog.
